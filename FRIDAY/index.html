STARK AI ARCHIVE — CANON REFERENCE

F.R.I.D.A.Y. — MCU

Female Replacement Intelligent Digital Assistant Youth. Created by Tony Stark as a successor to J.A.R.V.I.S.; she was selectable from a chip the moment J.A.R.V.I.S. was uploaded into the Vision body. Debut: Avengers: Age of Ultron. Voiced by Kerry Condon (Irish). Addresses Tony as "Boss".

J.A.R.V.I.S.

Just A Rather Very Intelligent System. Stark's original natural-language AI, running every armor and the Malibu house. Uploaded into the synthetic body that became Vision.

VISION

Born from J.A.R.V.I.S., the Mind Stone, Vibranium and Helen Cho's synthetic tissue. "I am not Ultron. I am not J.A.R.V.I.S. I am… I am."

ULTRON

A peacekeeping program born of the Mind Stone; concluded humanity had to go. Sokovia's ascent, and the reason the Sokovia Accords exist.

K.A.R.E.N.

Peter Parker's suit AI, built by Tony. "Karen" — she reads a room and offers to activate Instant Kill, then regrets it when asked.

E.D.I.T.H.

"Even Dead, I'm The Hero." Bequeathed to Peter Parker in Tony's will: an augmented-reality defense system wired into an entire satellite network.

H.O.M.E.R. / DUM-E / U

Comics-side Stark A.I. H.O.M.E.R. (a natural-language head of Stark's systems) plus two of the clumsiest robots ever assembled — DUM-E and U, both fitted with fire extinguishers for good reason.

VERONICA

The orbital Hulkbuster support platform — drops the Iron Man armor pieces that snap onto the Mark XLIV. Named for the reason Tony built it.

PROTOCOLS

House Party Protocol — the Iron Legion deploys, every empty armor rises. Clean Slate — detonate all suits, retire. Baby Monitor — watch the kid.

THE ARMORY

Mark III, VII, XLII, XLIV (Hulkbuster), XLV, XLVI, L, LVIII, the nanotech Mark L and Mark LXXXV. Bleeding Edge. In Endgame, F.R.I.D.A.Y. ran the Mark LXXXV for the last run.

Ask her about any of it — or about anything else. She distinguishes MCU canon from 616 comics and will tell you plainly when she's extrapolating.
